## South Westminster Legal Advice Centre — westminster
Candidate: discovery-17304270649819ef2df9. Source checked: 2026-11-09.
Category: Legal advice and rights
- What it offers: Volunteer lawyers provide legal advice for people on low incomes, including help understanding rights in disputes and non-contentious matters.
- Who: Primarily people who live or work in Westminster and cannot afford a solicitor, including homeless people with a Westminster connection.
- Access: Book an appointment through the provider website. The service does not currently accept drop-ins.
- Costs/checks: Advice is free, but the centre requests small donations for some letters, documents and photocopies; check whether it can advise on your issue before booking.
- Coverage: City of Westminster, with surrounding postcodes considered when resources permit
- Website: <https://swlac.org/>
- Evidence: The provider's current pages say volunteer lawyers give free advice to people who cannot afford legal help, primarily those living or working in Westminster, and require users to make an appointment online.
- Limitations: The centre gives advice rather than legal representation and depends on volunteer capacity. It requests small donations for certain written work or copies, and help outside Westminster is only offered when resources permit.
- Source: <https://swlac.org/get-help/who-we-help/> — The provider states its Westminster connection and low-income criteria, advice-only scope, free advice and small requested document or copying donations.
- Source: <https://swlac.org/> — The current home page confirms appointment-only access, the inability to accept drop-ins, free advice and service for people living or working in or near Westminster.
