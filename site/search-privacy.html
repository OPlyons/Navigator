How the support wizard uses your search

When you choose Search, Cloudflare Workers AI reads your words to help find matches in the Navigator directory. You can include a borough. Leave out names, addresses, dates of birth, NHS numbers, contact details and clinical notes.

The wizard suggests services. Each service decides who it can support and whether it has space.

With this option on, your words stay on this device. The tab remembers your choice when you return to the wizard.

This site does not save search text or conversation history, put searches in web addresses, or add search text to its application logs. Cloudflare processes requests under its own data arrangements. Technical fault reports sent to Sentry exclude search text, form entries and session recordings. Read Cloudflare’s AI data information.
