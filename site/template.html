Community AI Navigator and your search

Describe the kind of support needed and a borough. Do not enter anyone’s name, address, date of birth, NHS number, contact details or clinical notes.

Choosing “Find support” sends the search text to Cloudflare Workers AI to interpret the request. Service suggestions and contact details come from this site’s checked directory. AI does not make referrals, book services or check eligibility or live availability.

This site does not save search text or conversation history, put searches in web addresses, or add search text to its application logs. The AI and hosting provider processes requests under its own data arrangements. Read Cloudflare’s AI data information.

“Search without AI” uses local search in your browser and sends no search text to AI. Area and filter choices stay in the open page. Start again clears the search and suggestions.

Technical information

The site has no accounts, call records or visitor tracking. Search and filter choices stay in the open page. The hosting provider may keep technical records of visits.

Sentry receives limited technical error reports. Search text, form entries and session recordings are excluded.

Links to outside services use those services’ own privacy arrangements.
